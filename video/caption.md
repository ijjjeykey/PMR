Renting out your Dubai apartment as a holiday home? Read this before you sign anything 👇

A referral agent is a middleman. They introduce you to a management company and get paid for it: sometimes up to AED 5,000, sometimes a % of your rental income. Some of them charge you as well.

The management company already takes 15–25% of your rental revenue. You don't need anyone in between.

✅ Check the operator holds a Vacation Homes Rental licence
✅ Compare fees, terms and exit clauses
✅ Sign directly with the company
